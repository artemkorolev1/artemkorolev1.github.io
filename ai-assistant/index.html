ai-assistant

ai-assistant is a personal automation application developed and operated by Artem Korolev for his own use.

It is being developed to connect personal AI agents and workflows to Google services, including Gmail, Google Drive, and Google Calendar, using permissions granted through Google OAuth.

Its intended functions include finding information, organizing files, assisting with email, and managing calendar events. Available functions depend on the permissions granted and the workflows configured.

This application is not currently offered for public registration.
